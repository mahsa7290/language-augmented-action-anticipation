# Operational coding manual

- **Primary C1:** principal intervention changes perceptual, textual, cross-view, memory, or multimodal context supplied to the predictor.
- **Primary C2:** principal intervention infers, retrieves, or conditions on a goal, intention, subgoal, semantic prototype, or trajectory used as an intent proxy. Semantic conditioning without a normalised latent distribution is not coded as probabilistic goal inference.
- **Primary C3:** principal intervention changes the future-prediction objective, candidate scorer, sequence model, or decoding stability.
- **Primary C4:** principal intervention tests or constrains symbolic, geometric, physical, procedural, or embodied feasibility beyond ordinary label prediction. C4 is adjacent/emerging, not evidence-equivalent to C1-C3.
- **Secondary roles:** multi-label indicators record additional functions; primary labels are used only for counts.
- **Dedicated isolation:** an ablation or controlled contrast targets the coded intervention. **Partial** changes coupled elements. **None** means no isolating evidence was identified.
